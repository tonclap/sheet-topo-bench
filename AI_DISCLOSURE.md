# AI disclosure

This work was produced with substantial AI assistance (Anthropic Claude),
under human direction and review. The human owner set the target, the
protocol discipline (rules declared before runs, held-out split, negative
results published), made every irreversible decision — what to freeze, what to
publish, what to spend — and reviewed the results; the AI wrote code, ran the declared
experiments, and drafted the documentation.

This follows the Vesuvius Challenge community's disclosure practice for
AI-assisted contributions.
